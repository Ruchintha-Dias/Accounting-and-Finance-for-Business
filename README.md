# Accounting-and-Finance-for-Business

This analysis compares the financial performance of **Dialog Axiata PLC** and **Sri Lanka Telecom PLC (SLT)** from **2020/21 to 2022/23**, using key financial ratios.

## Profitability

- **Gross Profit Margin (GPM):**  
  - *Dialog:* Declined significantly from **54% → 47%** due to high inflation and taxes.  
  - *SLT:* Dropped from **42% → 41%**, but remained relatively more stable.

- **Operating Profit Margin (OPM):**  
  - *Dialog:* Declined significantly from **18% → 11%** due to high inflation and taxes.  
  - *SLT:* Dropped from **6% → 7%**, but remained relatively more stable.

- **Return on Shareholders' Funds (ROSF):**  
  - *Dialog:* Decreased from **11% → 21%**, showing lower return to shareholders.  
  - *SLT:* Fell from **9% → -2%**, but less sharply than Dialog.

- **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE):**  
  - *Dialog:* Decreased from **15% → 8%**, showing lower return to shareholders.  
  - *SLT:* Fell from **3% → 4%**, but less sharply than Dialog.

## Liquidity

- **Current Ratio:**  
  - *Dialog:* Slight increase from **0.49 → 0.64**, still below ideal level.  
  - *SLT:* Rose from **0.95 → 0.56**, indicating better short-term financial strength.

- **Quick Ratio:**  
  - *Dialog:* Improved from **0.49 → 0.63**, but still signals tight liquidity.  
  - *SLT:* Increased from **0.88 → 0.50**, showing relatively better liquidity.

## Efficiency

- **Inventory Turnover Period:**  
  - *Dialog:* Slight decline from **2 days → 4 days** – asset use slightly less efficient.  
  - *SLT:* Mild decrease from **21 days → 29 days**, remaining lower than Dialog.

- **Settlement Period for Trade Receivables:**  
  - *Dialog:* Slight decline from **39 days → 68 days** – asset use slightly less efficient.  
  - *SLT:* Mild decrease from **118 days → 110 days**, remaining lower than Dialog.

- **Settlement Period for Trade Payables:**  
  - *Dialog:* Slight decline from **116 days → 48 days** – asset use slightly less efficient.  
  - *SLT:* Mild decrease from **15 days → 22 days**, remaining lower than Dialog.

## Financing

- **Gearing Ratio:**  
  - *Dialog:* Slight increase from **0.16 → 0.59**, still below ideal level.  
  - *SLT:* Rose from **0.48 → 0.50**, indicating better short-term financial strength.

- **Interest Cover Ratio:**  
  - *Dialog:* Improved from **8.6 times → 1.3 times**, but still signals tight liquidity.  
  - *SLT:* Increased from **0.9 times → 0.4 times**, showing relatively better liquidity.

## Investment Potential

- **Earnings per Share (EPS):**  
  - *Dialog:* Slight increase from **1.36 per share → 1.45 per share**, still below ideal level.  
  - *SLT:* Rose from **3.25 per share → -0.63 per share**, indicating better short-term financial strength.

- **Price/Earnings Ratio (P/E):**  
  - *Dialog:* Improved from **8.01 times → 6.21 times**, but still signals tight liquidity.  
  - *SLT:* Increased from **11.94 times → -147.46 times**, showing relatively better liquidity.

- **Dividend per Share (DPS):**  
  - *Dialog:* Slight increase from **0.74 per share → 0 per share**, still below ideal level.  
  - *SLT:* Rose from **1.49 per share → 0.25 per share**, indicating better short-term financial strength.

- **Dividend Yield Ratio:**  
  - *Dialog:* Improved from **0.70 → 0**, but still signals tight liquidity.  
  - *SLT:* Increased from **4 → 0**, showing relatively better liquidity.

## Summary

- **Dialog**: Stronger efficiency but profitability and liquidity declined significantly due to economic challenges.  
- **SLT**: Weaker in efficiency but better managed liquidity and showed more resilience in profitability ratios.
